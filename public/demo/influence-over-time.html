Methodology B — citations by the date the words were said

Pin each citation to when the quote was actually published.

0 of 38 citations currently carry a publication date — so this chart cannot be drawn yet, and that absence is itself a finding. Kynda’s provenance today is dominated by Wikipedia harvests, which confirm that a thing was said but rarely when. Dated quotes live in interview archives — the NYT culture archive, NPR, ProQuest’s music-magazine collections — precisely the closed sources on the partnership shopping list. Honest methodology notes. (1) The signal date is the citing artist’s CAREER START (Wikidata work-period for people, formation for bands, creation year for works) — never birth, which smears people two decades early (John Foxx: born 1947, active 1970). Undocumented career starts are excluded, not guessed: Josef K, Karl Hyde, Kompressor, Kevin Saunderson. (2) Density: this is the corpus’s incidental signal — ~20 citations per artist accrued as byproducts of building other pages. Tony’s 2024 manual pass on one artist (Beyoncé) found 222 connections in Wikipedia alone and 550 with Setlist.fm; a deliberate “who cites X” sweep would bring that density here. (3) Sparse recent decades reflect missing documentation, not waning influence — newer artists have thinner archives (the 2024 deck’s own caution, kept).
